VIDEO ASSET DIRECTORY
======================

Place your video files here (e.g. showreel.mp4, geeky-gamer.mp4, commercial-ad.mp4).

To use a video:
1. Copy your .mp4 or .webm file into this folder: /public/videos/my-video.mp4
2. Open /src/data/portfolioData.ts
3. Set `videoUrl: "/videos/my-video.mp4"` for that project.
4. You can also paste YouTube or Vimeo embed URLs directly into `videoUrl`!

If videoUrl is empty or not yet provided, the portfolio automatically displays the high-fidelity video player simulation with interactive controls, timecode display, and scrubbing.
